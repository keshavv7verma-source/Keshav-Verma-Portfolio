About Me

I grew up in Gurgaon, Delhi NCR, with a deep curiosity and a simple belief: anything can be mastered with dedicated effort. That mindset shapes how I approach challenges. I keep learning, adapting, and looking at problems from different perspectives.

I’m a first-year undergraduate pursuing two degrees at once: a BBA (General) with a Finance Minor at Maharaja Surajmal Institute (IP University), and a BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. Alongside these, I’m preparing for the CFA Level I exam in August 2027. Together, they give me a well-rounded grounding in business frameworks, digital markets, and finance.

My interests lie at the intersection of finance and entrepreneurship. I want to start my career in a demanding corporate role where I can learn how industries work and solve real business problems. Over time, I aim to move into entrepreneurship and turn what I learn into ventures of my own.
